LLM: Grok 4.7 in Cursor.
Date: 1 November 2026.

The numbered Python scripts, README, and source notes were drafted in Cursor.
Official vote counts are downloaded from Elections Alberta. They were not
generated by the model.

The separation-poll table in data/01-raw_data/polls_separation.csv was copied
from the 338Canada page on 1 November 2026. A scripted request to that page
returned HTTP 406, so it was transcribed from the published table rather than
scraped. The three percentage columns are unlabeled on that page. They were
stored as remain, separation, and undecided because remain minus separation
matches the printed "Remain +N" lead.
